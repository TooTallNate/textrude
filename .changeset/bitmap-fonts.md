Bitmap fonts in AngelCode BMFont format: pass a `.fnt` file or URL (its page images are read from beside it) or a `.zip` of the `.fnt` and its pages, as `--font`, `font` or `fallbackFonts`. The glyphs are traced into outlines with `@tootallnate/bitmap-font`. The browser demo accepts a `.zip`, or a `.fnt` picked or dropped together with its pages.
